About

Ezra Zaafarani is the CEO of DGL Group Ltd (also known as DGL Group), a consumer electronics, appliances, and reverse logistics company founded in 2001 and headquartered in Edison, New Jersey. Under his leadership, DGL has grown into a multi-country business with a presence that includes Thailand and other markets, serving customers with products and logistics solutions at scale.

Beyond the company, Ezra is active in nonprofit healthcare work. He is a co-founder and Senior Case Manager of the Sephardic Division of Chaim Medical, and he serves as Treasurer of Sephardic Medical Resources Inc, a Brooklyn nonprofit. His work bridges day-to-day business operations with hands-on community service.

Leadership at DGL Group

At DGL Group, Ezra helps run the company across finance, payroll, and product hiring, working closely with his team. The firm operates in the consumer electronics and appliances space and provides reverse logistics services. Public estimates place DGL’s revenue in the $100–150 million range, with a team size reported between roughly 30 and 200 employees depending on the source.

Ezra’s role combines operational leadership with people and product decisions. He remains involved in hiring for product roles and in the financial and payroll systems that keep the organization running.

Community and Nonprofit Work

Ezra co-founded and serves as Senior Case Manager for the Sephardic Division of Chaim Medical. He is also Treasurer of Sephardic Medical Resources Inc (EIN 87-3994607), a Brooklyn nonprofit dedicated to medical resources for the community.

In February 2023, Community Magazine featured an inside view of Chaim Medical that included Ezra’s perspective on the organization’s work. That coverage reflects his ongoing commitment to practical help for families navigating healthcare needs.
